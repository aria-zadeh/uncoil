# distill-v12-study-memorise-b: the study-memorise slice of the v12 batches.
# Voice B (v12 addition 6): step titles 4 to 9 words in the student's own
# words, the first step always physical and 5 minutes, titles typed the way a
# student types them, descriptions in the student's words or a pasted teacher
# line.
#
# Quota (v12 addition 1): every one of the 55 subjects in the brief's table
# gets 9 records (495) and the remaining 5 go anywhere. Assignment types are
# restricted to studying for a test, memorising (vocabulary, formulas, lines,
# a piece of music, a speech), exam prep across several chapters, revision of
# returned work, and practice (an instrument, a sport skill, speaking a
# language, a routine). For those types the steps are still physical
# (addition 2): write the formula from memory, cover the column and say the
# words aloud, time yourself on the practice test.
#
# Sessions per 500 (addition 4): 15 min 85, 20 min 75, 25 min 75, 30 min 110,
# 45 min 70, 60 min 85. Minutes are per step, never per sum.
# Descriptions (addition 3): 45 percent none, 30 percent one line, 25 percent
# a teacher paragraph.
